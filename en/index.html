FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If you have any other question, just message us on WhatsApp.

Who posts the content?

We prepare the content and you post it on your own account from the Publishing Panel. Every item's time, caption and hashtags are ready, so it takes a few seconds.

Who takes the photos and videos?

We start from your existing photos and videos. We prefer real photos, because we want your customers to get to know the real you.

Which devices does it work on?

It opens in the browser on iPhone, Android phones, iPad, Android tablets and computers. You can add it to your home screen like an app if you like; there is nothing else to download.

Do you work with businesses outside Türkiye?

Our base is in Kocaeli, Türkiye, and we work remotely: you send us your photos and videos, and the content is uploaded to your Publishing Panel, so no face-to-face meeting is needed. The panel is available in English; message us to talk about the language of your captions.

What if I want to change something?

Just tell us, or tap “Request a change” in the panel. Once the panel is updated, the change appears at the same link.

How is the price set?

It depends on your business's needs and the number of items. Tell us briefly about your business on WhatsApp and we will send you a quote that fits.
