FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If you have any other question, just message us on WhatsApp.

Do I pay to see and try the sample site?

No. We prepare a sample site for your business; you try it on your phone for 7 days and tell us what you want changed. If you like it, we publish it with a yearly package; if not, you pay nothing.

What do I pay each year?

A single yearly fee. Domain, hosting, SSL certificate, backups and support are included; there are no extra bills during the year. The fee depends on your business's needs, and we agree on it together on WhatsApp.

Who makes the changes?

You change products, prices, photos, text and contact details yourself from your own panel on your phone; they appear on the site as soon as you save. Or message us on WhatsApp and we will do it.

Can't I build it myself with a site builder?

You can; but choosing the design, writing the text, preparing the photos and keeping track of the domain, hosting and security settings are then up to you. We deliver your site ready, with your own details; you only change what you want to change.

I already have a domain. Can I use it?

Yes. We connect a domain bought from another company too; if you need to change a setting, we show you step by step.

What if I don't have photos?

We start from the photos on your Instagram and the ones you have. Where something is missing we use suitable royalty-free photos and tell you so; you replace them as you add your own.

What happens if I want to leave?

You can choose not to renew at the end of the yearly term. When you leave, your site's text and photos are given to you as files, and we point your domain wherever you tell us.

Who posts the content in the Instagram package?

We prepare the content and you post it on your own account from the Publishing Panel. Every item's time, caption and hashtags are ready, so it takes a few seconds.

Do you work with businesses outside Türkiye?

Yes. Our base is in Kocaeli, Türkiye, and we work remotely: you send us your details and photos, and your site and content arrive in your panel, so no face-to-face meeting is needed. The panel is available in English.
